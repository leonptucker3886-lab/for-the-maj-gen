HEARTH HOLD

Three settlers, one fire, a wild valley. Mark trees and rocks, plant farms, raise huts and towers, and keep your people fed, rested and alive through the raider nights.

How to play
• Pick a tool, then tap the map. Settlers do the work on their own.
• 🪓/⛏ mark trees and rocks: settlers gather wood and stone. Drag to mark many.
• 🌾 Farms grow by day; settlers harvest +5 food. Each settler eats about 1 food a day.
• 🛖 Huts give 2 beds and +2 room. New settlers arrive at dawn if there's room and spare food.
• Raiders attack at dusk from day 3 (every night from day 10). 🗼 Towers shoot them, 🧱 walls slow them. ⚔ Rally sends everyone to fight.
• Hungry, tired settlers lose mood and may stop working. Protect the Hearth: if it falls, or everyone dies, the colony ends.
• Survive 30 days to win. Then keep going for a record.
