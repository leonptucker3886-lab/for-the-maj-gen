How to Play

1. Pick a building in the bottom bar, then tap a tile. Drag to pan; pinch or scroll to zoom.
2. 🏠 Homes bring residents. They need 🌾 food and jobs (🛍️ Shops, 🏭 Factories, 🏢 Offices).
3. Buildings only work if they touch a 🛣️ Road and sit inside a ⚡ Power Plant's radius.
4. 🌳 Parks raise nearby happiness; 🏭 Factories pollute nearby homes. 🚒 Fire Stations stop fires; 🏥 Clinics lift the whole city.
5. Taxes arrive every month. Use ⬆️ to upgrade (level 3 max) and 🔨 to demolish (50% refund).
6. You lose if money stays below −$500 for 3 months, or happiness drops to 0%.
7. Score = peak population. Each city earns ✦ Legacy for the shop.
