Drop onto a hostile moon with your 3-bot squad. Burn the hive nests, download the data cores, grab salvage — then hold the landing pad until the dropship arrives.

ARMORY

HOW TO PLAY

• Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to move.
• Right thumb: drag on the right half to aim and fire. Let go and you auto-fire at the nearest threat.
• Shock Pulse: knocks back and damages everything close.
• Orbital Strike: huge blast where you aim (or on the nearest enemy). Don't stand in it!
• Destroy 3 red nests and stand in 2 blue data cores until they finish downloading.
• Go to the green pad and tap CALL SHIP. Survive 60 s, then be on the pad when the ship lands.
• If you fall, a squad bot can revive you (2 revives). Lose and you keep only 25% of your salvage.
• Spend salvage in the Armory. Every win raises the threat level.
