COG WORKS

Mine glowing ore, smelt it, assemble gears and circuits, and sell them. Build a neon factory one conveyor at a time.

How to play
• Pick a part in the bar, then tap a tile. The arrow button sets the direction new parts face. Tap a placed part with the same tool to turn it.
• Drag with the Belt tool to draw conveyors.
• ⛏ Miners sit on ore and push it out the way they face.
• ♨ Smelters turn ore into plates.
• ⚙ Assemblers: 2 iron plates → gear; 1 iron + 2 copper plates → circuit (circuits come first when both are possible).
• $ Sellers buy anything: ore $1, plates $4, gears $10, circuits $28.
• Finish all 6 goals for big bonuses. Your factory auto-saves.
